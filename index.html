Safety First

No Shortcut
On Safety.
Ever.

Every FlameCore cylinder undergoes a rigorous 12-point safety check before it leaves our facility. We adhere to and exceed every BIS standard — because your family deserves nothing less.

  • Hydraulic pressure tested at 3× normal operating pressure
  • BIS IS:3196 compliance — mandatory for all cylinders
  • Dual-seal leak-proof valve with tamper evidence
  • Anti-corrosion coat rated for 20-year outdoor exposure
  • 24/7 emergency helpline with 15-minute response SLA

BIS / ISI Certified

All cylinders carry ISI mark as per IS:3196 — India's highest safety standard for LPG cylinders.

PESO Approved

Petroleum and Explosives Safety Organisation approval — mandatory for licensed LPG distribution.

ISO 9001:2015

Our quality management system is ISO certified — consistent processes across every cylinder we fill.

24/7 SLA Guarantee

Emergency response within 15 minutes, any time of day. Our safety hotline never sleeps.
